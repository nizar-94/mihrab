Prayer On On time Early Adjust Today
See what a prayer reminder looks like

On time notifies at the prayer time itself. Early adds a separate warning that many minutes beforehand — set both to get two notifications, or turn off “On time” to get only the warning. Adjust shifts a prayer by up to 59 minutes to match your local mosque.

Morning and evening adhkar

Set a location in the Athan tab to enable adhkar reminders.

Adhkar follow the prayer times rather than the clock, so the reminder stays in its proper window as the day length changes through the year.

Fasting reminders

Set a location in the Athan tab to enable fasting reminders.

Reminders arrive the day before, so there is still time to prepare and to make suhoor.
